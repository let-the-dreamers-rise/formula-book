# Market evidence from comparables

We have not spoken to a colourist yet. What follows is public evidence that salons and colourists already pay to record colour formulas. It shows the job is real and paid for. It does not show that anyone wants to do it by voice, and it does not show demand for Formula Book. Read 23 Oct 2026.

| Evidence | Source | What it shows | What it does not show |
|---|---|---|---|
| SalonScale says it is "trusted by over 8,000 salons" and lets stylists "instantly save and reuse your color formulas"; it works with a Bluetooth scale or "any scale you already have" | https://salonscale.com | Thousands of salons use software at the colour bar to weigh and save formulas | Its salon count is its own claim; no voice capture is mentioned |
| SalonScale closed a seed round of about $1 million | https://betakit.com/regina-based-salonscale-technology-closes-1-million-seed-round/ | Investors funded colour-bar software | Anything about voice |

## What we infer, and how we would check it

- Inference: if salons pay for scales and apps to save formulas, the record itself has value; the open question is whether speaking it is faster than tapping it with colour on the gloves.
- Check: the three questions in `docs/validation.md`, and a one-week trial with ten independent colourists recording every bowl by voice. The plan changes if they say they never record at the bowl or would not speak to a device.
