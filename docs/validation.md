# Colourist conversations

No conversations yet. When a message is sent, add a line `sent: <date> <channel> <role>`. When a reply comes, add the date, role, a verbatim quote (with permission) and what it changes. Never paraphrase into a quote.

The three questions (about past behaviour, not about the idea):

1. Tell me about the last time you needed a client's formula from a previous visit. Where did you find it, how long did it take, and what happened when it was missing or wrong?
2. Walk me through the bowl, from the first scoop to the end. When do you write the numbers down, and where: card, phone notes, salon app, memory? Do you weigh everything, and how do you scale a formula for more hair? How do you say a mix ratio out loud, and is developer measured by weight or by volume?
3. If a speaker at the backbar could save and read back formulas, would you talk to it with colour on your gloves? What would make you refuse? And what do you pay for salon software each month today?

What would change the plan: if all three say they never record numbers at the bowl, or would never talk to a speaker, the README drops the hands-busy claim and leads with per-client memory and scaling only.
